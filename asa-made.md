**ASA MADE — 朝まで**

**Meaning:** “Until morning” or “until dawn.”

It suggests driving through the entire night and continuing until the city begins to wake up. For the game, it carries a feeling of endless night roads, quiet expressways, fatigue, freedom, and chasing one more lap before sunrise.

**Pronunciation:**

**ah-sah mah-deh**

Japanese pronunciation:

**あさまで**
**a-sa ma-de**

Each syllable is short and even:

**AH-sah MAH-deh**

* **asa — 朝** = morning
* **made — まで** = until, up to

A natural English-style description for the title:

> **ASA MADE** means “Until Morning” — a night that continues through empty roads, city lights, and repeated laps until dawn.
